Hack Club Wheaton is the student-run coding club at WIS. Part of Hack Club, a nonprofit with 400+ clubs and 104K+ coders worldwide. Think of it as the friend group for people who build things.

Zero marks. Zero pressure. You show up, someone kicks off a project, you build whatever you want, and everyone shows what they made at the end.

1
Intro — leader kicks things off to get everyone started.

Build — make whatever you want: website, game, app. Your call.

Demo — everyone shows what they made. No judgement, just good vibes.
